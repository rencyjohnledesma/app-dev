# Fury (2014)

**Genre:** War, Action, Drama  
**Director:** David Ayer  
**Main Cast:** Brad Pitt, Shia LaBeouf, Logan Lerman, Jon Bernthal, Michael Peña

---

## Overview
Fury is a World War II film that follows a Sherman tank crew led by Don "Wardaddy" Collier. Despite being outnumbered and exhausted, the crew continues to fight through impossible situations.

---

## Main Characters
- Don "Wardaddy" Collier – Tank commander  
- Boyd "Bible" Swan – Gunner  
- Norman Ellison – New recruit  
- Trini "Gordo" Garcia – Driver  
- Grady Travis – Loader

---

## Why I Like It
> The film shows the brutality of war, the loyalty within the crew, and the intense atmosphere of every battle.

---

## Favorite Scene
The final stand against a large group of German soldiers, where the crew decides to fight until the very end.

---

## Additional Notes
- The tank named "Fury" is an actual Sherman tank used during production.  
- The movie emphasizes sacrifice, brotherhood, and the psychological weight carried by soldiers in wartime.
